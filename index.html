01. Strategy & Problem Space

Moving Beyond Unanchored "AI Aesthetics"

Most AI-generated e-commerce imagery fails because it treats products as decorative props: hallucinating nonexistent packaging labels, over-decorating scenes with messy leaves, or inventing unsupported clinical claims.

The Strategy: Take the physical truth of KORA's pale sage-green tube, its gold botanical crest, and its stated neem and green tea formulation, and build a modular campaign world around restraint, clarity, and genuine commercial utility.

05. Production Architecture

Prompt Engineering & Generation Controls

Rather than vague aesthetic adjectives ("hyperrealistic, 8k, luxury"), commercial creative direction defines explicit physical lighting coordinates, optical lens lengths, material surface physics, and strict negative constraints.

v2.4 Production

// Commercial Hero Prompt Blueprint

Environment: Unpolished travertine limestone plinth in warm 7:30 AM Mediterranean sunlight, gentle raking shadows at 25-degree elevation.

Subject: KORA Beauty Purifying Gel Cleanser in exact pale sage-green (#C2CBB9) tube, matte finish, white flip-top cap, upright composition.

Botanical Props: Three crisp fresh green neem leaves, dried rolled green tea leaves, fine micro water beads on stone edge.

Optics: Hasselblad 90mm macro lens, f/4 aperture for gentle background falloff, zero plastic studio strobe glare.

// Sensory Gel Macro Prompt Blueprint

Subject: Microscopic cross-section of translucent skincare gel droplet resting on wet porous ivory stone.

Internal Optics: Visible suspended micro air bubbles, subtle botanical herbal green tint, natural refractive light caustics.

Depth of Field: Extreme macro 100mm f/2.8, razor-thin focal plane on crown of droplet, zero chromatic aberration.

// Negative Tokens & Boundary Constraints

Strict Negatives: distorted packaging typography, hallucinated foreign language text, floating impossible leaves, high-gloss plastic sheen, oily specular blowout, fake clinical cross icons, deformed anatomical hands, muddy color cast, artificial oversaturation.

Enforcement: Verified through multi-pass inpainting and vector typography overlay where required.

06. The Discipline

Product Truth & Claim Verification

Anyone can generate generic glossy beauty imagery with modern generative models. What separates junior prompting from senior creative direction is rigorous claim verification and physical packaging fidelity.

1. Packaging Integrity ✓
  • • Pack Hue: Pale sage-green tube with matte off-white flip-top cap strictly maintained.
  • • Branding: Gold botanical leaf crest and dark-green typography aligned with official packaging.
  • • Volume: Stated net quantity 100 ml / 3.38 fl. oz. preserved.
2. Zero False Claims ✓
  • • No Medical Claims: No unsubstantiated acne cures or instant wrinkle reversal claims.
  • • True Statements: Only cited "Dermatologically Tested", "Sulphate Free", "Paraben Free" from pack.
  • • Ingredient Truth: Restricted botanical storytelling strictly to Neem and Green Tea.
3. Commercial Viability ✓
  • • Mobile Legibility: Benefit labels structured for rapid comprehension on small viewports.
  • • Negative Space: Compositions leave intentional breathing room for e-commerce headlines.
  • • Multi-Channel: Assets ready for PDP carousels, paid social ads, and hero banners.
